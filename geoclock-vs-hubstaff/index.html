The Core Pricing Difference

Hubstaff's pricing starts low per user but compounds fast. At their standard tier, a 10-person crew costs $70–$100+/mo. At 15 employees you're looking at $105–$150+/mo. Those numbers grow every time you hire someone new.

GeoClock charges $39.99/mo for up to 10 employees and $59.99/mo for up to 15 — flat, predictable, and significantly less for most contractor crew sizes.

Hubstaff's Strengths

Hubstaff is a feature-rich platform built for remote teams and knowledge workers as much as field crews. It includes screenshot capture, app usage monitoring, project tracking, invoicing, and integrations with dozens of project management tools. If you need those capabilities, Hubstaff delivers them.

Where GeoClock Wins for Contractors

For a contractor whose primary need is GPS-verified job site time tracking, GeoClock is purpose-built and significantly cheaper. Hubstaff's additional features — screenshots, app monitoring, project tracking — add cost but provide little value on a construction job site.

GeoClock also wins on simplicity. No app installation, no employee accounts, 10-minute setup. Hubstaff is a full workforce management platform that takes time to configure and train.
