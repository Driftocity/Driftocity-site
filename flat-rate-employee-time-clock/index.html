Per-User Pricing vs Flat-Rate Pricing — What's the Difference?

Per-user pricing means you pay a monthly fee for each employee on your account. It sounds reasonable at first — maybe $7 or $8 per user per month. But the math changes fast when your crew grows.

Flat-rate pricing means you pay one monthly fee regardless of how many employees you have (up to your plan limit). Your costs are predictable and don't punish you for growing your team.
