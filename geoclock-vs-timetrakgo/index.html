Pricing Comparison

TimeTrakGO charges per user per month. For small crews of 2–3 people it may be comparable in price to GeoClock. But as your crew grows, the per-user model becomes increasingly expensive. A 10-person crew on TimeTrakGO costs $50–$80+/mo vs $39.99/mo flat with GeoClock.

No-App Advantage

TimeTrakGO requires employees to download and install an app. GeoClock runs in any mobile browser — no download, no account, no friction. For field crews this is a meaningful practical advantage.

When TimeTrakGO Might Be a Better Fit

TimeTrakGO has been in the market longer and has more established integrations with payroll platforms. If you need deep payroll software integration (ADP, QuickBooks, etc.), TimeTrakGO may be worth exploring. GeoClock focuses on doing GPS time tracking simply and well at a lower cost.

Is GeoClock cheaper than TimeTrakGO?

For crews of 5 or more employees, yes. GeoClock's flat pricing is significantly lower than TimeTrakGO's per-user model at larger crew sizes.

What is a good TimeTrakGO alternative?

GeoClock is a strong TimeTrakGO alternative for contractors who want flat-rate GPS geofence time tracking without requiring employees to install an app.

Does GeoClock integrate with payroll software?

GeoClock exports timesheets as CSV and PDF which can be imported into most payroll platforms. Direct API integrations are on the roadmap.
