Our Story

Built Lean. Shipped Fast.

We’re Driftocity — an independent digital products company that builds tools and guides for people who don’t have time to waste.

Driftocity exists because most software is over-engineered and most guides are padded thin. We set out to fix both of those things — one product at a time.

We build AI-powered SaaS tools that solve real problems: tracking employee time with GPS enforcement, writing business plans, generating project estimates. And we write PDF guides that actually go deep — not 10 pages of obvious advice, but dense, practical frameworks built from hard-won experience.

Everything we make is designed to deliver value immediately. You buy, you access, you use. No onboarding calls, no account setup headaches, no waiting.
