Pricing: The Biggest Difference

ClockShark charges per user per month. This means every new hire increases your monthly bill. For a 10-person crew, you're paying $70–$90+ per month with ClockShark. With GeoClock's Team plan, you pay $39.99/mo flat for the same crew — saving $30–$50+ every month.

For a 15-person crew the gap grows further: ClockShark runs $105–$135+/mo vs GeoClock's $59.99/mo flat. That's over $900/year in savings.

App Requirement: A Real Operational Difference

ClockShark requires employees to download and install an app and create login accounts. This creates friction — especially with older workers, part-time crew members, or anyone resistant to installing new software on their personal phone.

GeoClock runs in any mobile browser. Your crew gets a link, they open it, they tap their name. No download, no account, no friction.

GPS Enforcement: Flags vs Hard Blocks

ClockShark tracks GPS location and can flag when employees clock in outside a geofence — but it does not hard-block clock-in. An employee outside your job site can still punch in and the system records a flag.

GeoClock hard-blocks clock-in outside the geofence. If an employee is not inside the boundary, they cannot clock in. Period.

When ClockShark Might Be a Better Fit

ClockShark is a mature platform with more features — job costing, scheduling, and richer reporting. If you need those features specifically, it may be worth the higher price. GeoClock is purpose-built for GPS enforcement and time tracking — it does those things extremely well at a much lower cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GeoClock cheaper than ClockShark?

For most contractor crews of 5 or more employees, yes — significantly. GeoClock's flat pricing becomes increasingly advantageous as your crew grows.

Does GeoClock have the same features as ClockShark?

GeoClock focuses on GPS geofence enforcement, time tracking, and payroll export. ClockShark has additional features like job costing and scheduling. GeoClock is the better choice if your primary need is accurate, enforced GPS time tracking at lower cost.

Can I switch from ClockShark to GeoClock easily?

Yes. GeoClock setup takes under 10 minutes. You export your existing employee list and add them to GeoClock.
