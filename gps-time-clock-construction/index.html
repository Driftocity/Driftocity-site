Why Contractors Specifically Need GPS Time Tracking

Office employees work in one place. Contractors work everywhere — different job sites, different crews, different days. That complexity makes time tracking harder and fraud easier.

The problems contractors face:

🚗 Crew clocks in before they arrive

Without GPS enforcement, nothing stops an employee from clocking in from their driveway or the highway. You pay for the drive time.

👥 Buddy punching

One crew member clocks in for someone who called in sick. You pay a full day for someone who never showed up.

📋 Paper timesheets you can't verify

Employees fill out their own hours at the end of the week. Memory is generous. You overpay every time.

⏰ No visibility into who's on site

You're running multiple jobs. You have no way to know if your crew actually showed up without calling around.

How GeoClock Fixes It

You set a GPS geofence around each job site. Your crew gets a browser link — no app to download. When they try to clock in, GeoClock checks their GPS coordinates. Inside the fence? They're in. Outside? Blocked. You see everything live on your dashboard.
