Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Driftocity ("we," "us," "our") collects when you visit driftocity.com or use our products, including EstimatePro and GeoClock, and how that information is used.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and phone number when you contact us, request a quote, or create an account for a SaaS product. When you purchase a product, our payment processor (Stripe) collects payment information directly — we do not store your card details.

We also automatically collect basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used for security and to understand site usage.

For SaaS tools like GeoClock, we collect the data necessary to operate the service, including employee names entered by an admin and GPS location data submitted at clock-in, used solely to verify geofence compliance for that account.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to deliver purchased products, respond to inquiries, process payments, maintain and improve our services, and communicate with you about your account or order.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate (such as Stripe for payments and our hosting provider), or when required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and order information for as long as needed to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing us.

7. Cookies

Our site may use basic cookies for functionality and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work as intended.

8. Children's Privacy

Driftocity's products are not directed at individuals under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
