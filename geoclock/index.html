FAQ

Common Questions

Does my crew need to download an app?

No. GeoClock runs in any mobile browser. You send them a link, they open it, tap their name, tap In. That's it.

What happens if someone tries to clock in from outside the geofence?

They're blocked. They see a message telling them they're not inside the job site boundary. No override, no workaround.

Why flat pricing instead of per user?

Because per-user pricing punishes you for growing your crew. With GeoClock you pay one flat rate and can add employees as you hire without watching your bill climb.

How long does setup take?

Under 10 minutes. Create your account, add your job site, add employee names, send the clock-in link. Done.

Can I use it on multiple job sites?

Yes. You can set up multiple geofences for different locations and assign employees to the right site.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel from your account settings whenever you want.
