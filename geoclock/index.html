What is GeoClock?

GeoClock is a GPS geofence time clock built for contractors, construction companies, and field service businesses. It replaces paper timesheets, punch clocks, and honor-system time tracking with GPS-verified clock-ins that only work when employees are physically on site.

Unlike competing tools that charge per employee per month, GeoClock uses flat monthly pricing — one rate for your whole crew, no matter how many people you add.

Who is GeoClock designed for?

General ContractorsElectrical Contractors Plumbing CompaniesHVAC Businesses Landscaping CrewsRoofing Companies Construction CompaniesField Service Businesses

Any business where employees work at a physical job site and clock-in accuracy matters. GeoClock is especially valuable for businesses with multiple crew members, multiple job sites, or a history of buddy punching and inflated hours.

How does GeoClock verify an employee's location?

When you set up GeoClock, you drop a pin on your job site and set a geofence radius. When an employee tries to clock in, the app checks their GPS coordinates against that boundary. If they're inside — they can clock in. If they're outside — they're blocked. The check happens in real time every time someone punches in.

Does GeoClock require employees to install an app?

No. GeoClock runs entirely in a mobile web browser. You send employees a clock-in link — they open it, tap their name, tap In. No app store, no download, no account creation required. This is one of GeoClock's biggest advantages over competitors like Hubstaff and ClockShark, both of which require app installation.
