FAQ

Common Questions

Do employees need to download an app?

No. GeoClock runs in any mobile browser. Your crew opens the link you text them, picks their name, and taps In. No app store, no download.

What stops buddy punching?

The GPS check runs the moment someone taps clock-in. Their device reports its location, GeoClock compares it to your geofence. If they’re outside the boundary, the request is blocked entirely.

What if an employee turns off their GPS?

If location access is denied, GeoClock can’t verify their position — and without verification, clock-in is blocked. Employees must have location enabled to use the app.

How does payroll export work?

From your admin dashboard, choose the date range and employee, hit Export. GeoClock generates a clean timesheet with name, date, clock-in/out times, and total hours. Download as CSV or PDF.

Can I change plans as my crew grows?

Yes. Start on Starter, upgrade to Team when you hire more. Data carries over. Cancel, upgrade, or downgrade anytime with no penalty.
