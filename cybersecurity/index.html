FAQ

Common Questions

I think I've been hacked — what should I do right now?

Disconnect from the internet immediately (unplug ethernet or disable WiFi). Do not turn the computer off — you may destroy forensic evidence. Do not pay any ransom. Call (765) 553-1097 right away. The faster we respond, the more we can limit the damage.

Should I pay the ransom if I'm hit with ransomware?

No — call us first. Paying the ransom does not guarantee you'll get your files back, funds criminal operations, and may make you a repeat target. We'll assess your situation and determine the best recovery path, which often doesn't require payment.

How do you work remotely on my computer?

We use secure, encrypted remote desktop tools that you initiate and control. You can see everything we're doing in real time and disconnect at any moment. We never have unmonitored access to your system.

Do you work with businesses or just individuals?

Both. We serve individuals, families, small businesses, contractors, medical offices, retailers, and any organization that needs cybersecurity help. Pricing scales with the size and complexity of the engagement.

Can you help with a cyber insurance claim?

Yes. We provide full written documentation of the incident, forensic findings, scope of breach, and remediation steps — all formatted for cyber insurance claims. This documentation can also be provided to law enforcement if needed.

What is the free assessment?

Before quoting any work, we'll talk through what happened, ask diagnostic questions, and give you an honest assessment of the situation. This initial consultation is always free. You get a firm price before any paid work begins.

My email was hacked — how bad is it?

Compromised email is serious because attackers use it to reset passwords on every other account linked to that email. They may also be reading your messages, impersonating you, or setting up forwarding rules to monitor future emails. Contact us as soon as possible — the longer access persists, the deeper the damage.

Do you handle HIPAA compliance for medical offices?

Yes. We perform security assessments aligned with HIPAA requirements, identify gaps in your current controls, and implement the technical safeguards required for compliance. We also provide documentation for audits.
