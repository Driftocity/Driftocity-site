Why No-App Time Tracking Matters for Field Crews

Getting a construction crew to install an app is harder than it sounds. Some employees have old phones with limited storage. Some are resistant to new technology. Some simply won't do it. Every person who doesn't install the app is a person you can't track.

GeoClock solves this by running entirely in a mobile web browser — the same browser your crew already uses every day. You send them a link, they bookmark it, they tap it every morning. That's the entire process.

How Browser-Based Clock-In Works

1. You set up your account and geofence

Takes about 10 minutes. You get a unique clock-in link for your crew.

2. You text or email the link to your crew

They save it as a bookmark on their phone home screen. Looks and works like an app — without the installation.

3. They open the link and tap their name

No login, no password, no account required for crew members. They just tap their name from the list.

4. GPS verification happens automatically

The browser checks their GPS location against your geofence. Inside = clocked in. Outside = blocked.

5. You see it live on your dashboard

Their clock-in appears instantly on your admin dashboard with timestamp and location confirmation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does GPS work in a browser without an app?

Yes. Modern mobile browsers support GPS location access through the Web Geolocation API. When your employee opens the clock-in link, the browser requests location permission once and uses it for GPS verification.

Is browser-based GPS as accurate as an app?

Yes. Browser GPS uses the same device GPS hardware as native apps. Accuracy is identical.

What if an employee denies location permission?

If location permission is denied, clock-in is blocked. The employee must allow location access to use GeoClock.

Can employees save the link to their home screen?

Yes. On both Android and iPhone, employees can add the link to their home screen where it functions like an app icon. Most crew members do this after the first day.
