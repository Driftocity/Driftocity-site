Why Your Small Business Website Is Costing You Customers (And How to Fix It)

By Raymond Hockersmith  ·  June 2025  ·  5 min read

Your website is your 24/7 salesperson. If it's slow, outdated, or hard to use on a phone, it's quietly sending potential customers straight to your competition — and you'll never know.

Problem 1: It's Not Mobile-Friendly

Over 60% of searches happen on a phone. If your site doesn't look good on mobile, Google ranks it lower AND visitors leave immediately. Check yours right now on your phone.

Problem 2: It Loads Too Slowly

If your site takes more than 3 seconds to load, 53% of visitors leave before they see anything. Google also penalizes slow sites in search rankings. Large images and bloated code are usually the culprits.

Problem 3: There's No Clear Call to Action

Every page needs to tell visitors what to do next — call you, fill out a form, buy something. If it's not obvious, people do nothing.

Problem 4: You Can't Be Found on Google

A pretty website that Google can't find is just a brochure no one reads. SEO basics — proper page titles, meta descriptions, and a Google Business Profile — make a massive difference.
