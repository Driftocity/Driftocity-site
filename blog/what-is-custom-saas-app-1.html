What Is a Custom SaaS App and Does Your Business Actually Need One?

By Raymond Hockersmith  ·  July 2025  ·  6 min read

SaaS stands for Software as a Service — basically, a web app you access through a browser. Gmail is SaaS. Salesforce is SaaS. But custom SaaS means an app built specifically for YOUR workflow, not a generic product you adapt yourself.

When Does a Custom App Make Sense?

You might need a custom app if you're spending hours on tasks that follow the same pattern every time — creating estimates, generating reports, answering the same customer questions, or moving data between spreadsheets.

What Does It Actually Cost?

Big agencies charge $20k–$100k for custom software. But a lean builder like Driftocity can build focused tools — that do one thing really well — for a fraction of that. Many of our custom builds fall in the $500–$2,500 range depending on complexity.

Real Examples of Small Business Custom Tools

Contractor estimating tool that generates line-item quotes in 60 seconds. Client portal where customers can view job status and approve proposals. AI-powered FAQ bot that answers customer questions on your website automatically.

How to Know if You're Ready

Ask yourself: Is there a repetitive task your team does that takes more than 2 hours per week? If yes, the right tool pays for itself in a month.
