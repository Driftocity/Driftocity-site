GPS Time Tracking for Contractors: Stop Paying for Hours Your Crew Wasn't There

By Raymond Hockersmith · August 2026 · 6 min read

If you're running a crew and relying on paper timesheets or an honor system to track hours, you're almost certainly losing money. Not because your crew is dishonest — but because the system makes it easy to clock in from the truck, round up to the nearest half hour, or forget to clock out.

What Buddy Punching Actually Costs

Buddy punching is when one employee clocks in for another who isn't on site yet — or isn't coming at all. Studies put the cost at around 2-3% of gross payroll. For a contractor running a $500,000 payroll, that's $10,000-$15,000 a year walking out the door.

And that's just buddy punching. Add in early clock-ins, late clock-outs, and rounding, and the number gets bigger fast.

How GPS Geofencing Fixes It

A geofence is a virtual boundary around your job site. You set it up once — drop a pin on the location, set the radius — and the system enforces it automatically. If an employee tries to clock in from outside that boundary, they're blocked. No exceptions, no overrides.

They can't clock in from the truck down the street. They can't clock in from home. They have to be physically inside the fence to punch in.

What to Look for in a GPS Time Tracking App

No app download required: Your crew isn't going to install another app. Browser-based clock-in means they open a link, tap their name, done.

Flat pricing: Per-user pricing sounds cheap until you have 10 employees and you're paying $8 per person per month. Flat monthly pricing means you know exactly what you're paying.

Easy payroll export: CSV or PDF export in one click. No manual tallying at the end of the week.

Live dashboard: See who's clocked in right now from your phone. No more calling around to find out if the crew showed up.

Setup Should Take 10 Minutes, Not 10 Days

A good GPS time tracking tool should be live the same day you sign up. Create your account, add your job site, add your employees by name, send them the clock-in link. That's it. If it takes longer than that, the tool is too complicated for a field crew.
